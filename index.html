About the app

Hebrew Master brings three Hebrew video courses together in one app. Choose a course from the main menu; each course has its own Home screen, progress and Settings & About, and a "Main menu" link at the top of its Home screen takes you back. The app runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.4 or later.

  • Hebrew For Beginners: the Hebrew alphabet, the vowel signs, reading, vocabulary, first sentences and handwriting, with three endless practices (alphabet, reading and handwriting). Sections 1 to 3 are free.
  • Hebrew Verbs: roots and the seven verb patterns (Binyanim), with Infinite Practice of 98 recorded verbs. The Intro to Hebrew Verbs and the Paal section are free.
  • Read Hebrew Without Vowel Signs: reading Hebrew the way Israelis write it, without vowel signs, with Infinite Reading Practice of 298 words. Sections 1 and 2 are free.

Each course has its own one-time purchase that unlocks the rest of that course permanently on every device signed in to your Apple Account. Buying one course does not unlock the others. No subscription.

Common questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All videos, readings, quizzes, pictures and recordings of the three courses are inside the app, so everything works without an internet connection. Nothing needs to download on first launch. The app is large (about 1.5 GB to download and 1.7 GB on your device) because the videos are included, so your iPhone may ask before downloading it over a cellular connection.

How do I keep my progress on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on both devices. The app saves each course's progress on the device and syncs it through your own iCloud account. Syncing can take a few minutes, and it waits until the device is online. There is no separate app account.

I bought a course. How do I unlock it on another device?

Use the same Apple Account for App Store purchases. The course usually unlocks by itself. If it does not, open that course's Settings & About (the gear on the course's Home screen) and tap Restore Purchases.

Can my family share a purchase?

No. The course purchases do not support Family Sharing.

The practice did not play a sound.

In the practices the recording plays after you answer, and Replay plays it again. Recordings play even when the ring/silent switch is on silent; check the volume of your device.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is granted, the paid part of that course locks again on your devices once the App Store reports it; the other courses are not affected. Your progress is kept.

What happens if I switch to a different iCloud account?

Each iCloud account keeps its own progress. Switching back to the earlier account brings its progress back.
